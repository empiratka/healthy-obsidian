## High calorie breakfast
Type: [[behavior]]

## Effects
- High-calorie breakfast with reduced intake at dinner with [[behavior_meal_restriction_calorie|calorie restriction]] is beneficial and might be a useful alternative for the management of [[condition_obesity|obesity]] and [[condition_metabolic_syndrome|metabolic syndrome]][^1]
	- The high calorie breakfast group showed greater [[metric_weight|weight]] loss and [[metric_waist_circumference|waist circumference]] reduction than [[behavior_meal_distribution_dinner_high_calorie|high calorie dinner]] group.
	- Fasting [[metric_fasting_glucose|glucose]], [[metric_fasting_insulin|insulin]], and [[metric_HOMA-IR|HOMA-IR]] decreased significantly to a greater extent in the high calorie breakfast group than [[behavior_meal_distribution_dinner_high_calorie|high calorie dinner]] group.
	- Mean [[metric_triglyceride_levels|triglyceride levels]] decreased by 33.6% in the high calorie breakfast group, but increased by 14.6% in the [[behavior_meal_distribution_dinner_high_calorie|high calorie dinner]] group.
	- Mean [[outcome_satiety|satiety]] scores were significantly higher in the high calorie breakfast group

[^1]: [[23512957]]
