## Breakfast omission
Пропуск завтрака

Type: [[behavior]]

- associated with higher prevalence of atherosclerosis[^2]

## Effects
- Men who skipped breakfast had a 27% higher risk of [[condition_coronary_heart_disease|CHD]].[^3]
	- These associations were mediated by [[metric_anthropometric_body_mass_index|BMI]], [[condition_hypertension|hypertension]], [[condition_hypercholesterolemia|hypercholesterolemia]] and [[condition_diabetes|diabetes]].
- After adjustment for known risk factors for [[condition_diabetes_type2|T2DM]], including [[metric_anthropometric_body_mass_index|BMI]], men who skipped breakfast had 21% higher risk of [[condition_diabetes_type2|T2DM]] than did men who consumed breakfast.[^1]
	- These findings persisted after stratification by [[metric_anthropometric_body_mass_index|BMI]] or [[diet_quality|diet quality]].
