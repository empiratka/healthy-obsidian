## Late-night eating
Приём пищи поздно вечером или ночью

Type: [[behavior]]
### Effects
- Compared with men who did not eat late at night, those who [[behavior_meal_distribution_dinner_late|ate late]] at night had a 55% higher [[condition_coronary_heart_disease|CHD]] risk.[^1] 
	- These associations were mediated by [[metric_anthropometric_body_mass_index|BMI]], [[condition_hypertension|hypertension]], [[condition_hypercholesterolemia|hypercholesterolemia]] and [[condition_diabetes|diabetes]].
