## Intermittent fasting
Интервальное голодание

Type: [[behavior]]

Description:
Eating pattern characterized by alternating periods of fasting and eating.

Associated effects:
- may be used in patients with [[condition_diabetes_type2|T2DM]] and [[condition_obesity|obesity]][^1]

[^1]: [[Time-Restricted Feeding and Intermittent Fasting as Preventive Therapeutics]]
