# Alternate-day fasting
Голодание через день

Type: [[behavior]]

Description:
Pattern involving alternating fasting days and eating days.

Variants:
- 24-hour fasting followed by 24-hour eating periods
- 5:2 approach (two fasting days, five non-restrictive days)

Notes:
Sometimes referred to as periodic prolonged fasting (PF) or intermittent calorie restriction (ICR), depending on protocol.
