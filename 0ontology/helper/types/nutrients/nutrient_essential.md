**Essential nutrients** are [dietary compounds](nutrient.md) that are required for normal physiological function and cannot be synthesized by the human body in sufficient amounts. A lack of essential nutrients leads to specific deficiency states or diseases.

They are defined by dietary indispensability, not by their biological function.
