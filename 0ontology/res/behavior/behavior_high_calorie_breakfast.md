Type: [[behavior]]

Effects:
- Improves metabolic outcomes compared with a high-calorie dinner in [[context_sex_female|women]] with [[condition_metabolic_syndrome]]:[^1]
	  - greater [[weight_loss]] and [[waist_circumference]] reduction
	  - improved [[glucose_metabolism]] and [[insulin_sensitivity]] (↓ fasting glucose, insulin, [[metric_homa_ir|HOMA-IR]])
	  - ↓ [[triglycerides]] (decrease vs increase in high-calorie dinner pattern)
	  - ↓ [[hunger]] and ↑ [[satiety]] across the day
