# Late-night eating
Приём пищи поздно вечером или ночью

Type: [[behavior]]

Associated effects:
- associated with increased risk of coronary heart disease (CHD)[^1]
	  - ~55% higher risk
	  - mediated by:
	    - [[metric_body_mass_index|BMI]]
	    - [[condition_hypertension|hypertension]]
	    - [[condition_hypercholesterolemia|hypercholesterolemia]]
	    - [[condition_diabetes_type2|diabetes]]

[^1]: [[A Prospective Study of Breakfast Eating and Incident Coronary Heart Disease in a Cohort of Male U.S. Health Professionals]]
