# Eating frequency
Частота приёмов пищи

Type: [[behavior]]

Associated effects:
- no association with coronary heart disease (CHD)[^1]

Notes:
Eating frequency refers to the number of eating occasions per day and does not capture timing of food intake.

[^1]: [[A Prospective Study of Breakfast Eating and Incident Coronary Heart Disease in a Cohort of Male U.S. Health Professionals]]
