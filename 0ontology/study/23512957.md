## High caloric intake at breakfast vs. dinner differentially influences weight loss of overweight and obese women

type: [[randomized_controlled_trial|RCT]]
### Population
- Overweight and [[condition_obesity|obese]] [[context_sex_female|women]] ([[metric_body_mass_index|BMI]] 32.4 ± 1.8 kg/m(2) ) with [[condition_metabolic_syndrome|metabolic syndrome]]
## Intervention characteristics
- Isocaloric (~1400 kcal) weight loss ([[behavior_meal_restriction_calorie|calorie restriction]])
- Were randomized into two groups, a [[behavior_meal_distribution_breakfast_high_calorie|breakfast]] (BF) (700 kcal breakfast, 500 kcal lunch, 200 kcal dinner) or a [[behavior_meal_distribution_dinner_high_calorie|dinner]] (D) group (200 kcal breakfast, 500 kcal lunch, 700 kcal dinner)
## Duration
- 12 weeks
## Findings
- The [[behavior_meal_distribution_breakfast_high_calorie|high calorie breakfast]] group showed greater [[metric_weight|weight]] loss and [[metric_waist_circumference|waist circumference]] reduction than [[behavior_meal_distribution_dinner_high_calorie|high calorie dinner]] group.
- Fasting [[metric_fasting_glucose|glucose]], [[metric_fasting_insulin|insulin]], and [[metric_HOMA-IR|HOMA-IR]] decreased significantly to a greater extent in the [[behavior_meal_distribution_breakfast_high_calorie|high calorie breakfast]] group than [[behavior_meal_distribution_dinner_high_calorie|high calorie dinner]] group.
- Mean [[metric_triglyceride_levels|triglyceride levels]] decreased by 33.6% in the [[behavior_meal_distribution_breakfast_high_calorie|high calorie breakfast]] group, but increased by 14.6% in the [[behavior_meal_distribution_dinner_high_calorie|high calorie dinner]] group
- Mean [[outcome_satiety|satiety]] scores were significantly higher in the BF group
