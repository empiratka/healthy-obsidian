## Meal Timing and Frequency: Implications for Cardiovascular Disease Prevention

type: [[study_design_systematic_review|systematic review]]
### Findings
- [[behavior_meal_frequency|Meal Frequency]]
	- Larger-scale and longer-term controlled trials will be required before any definitive conclusions can be reached on the impact of meal frequency on these cardiometabolic risk parameters. Each of trials has several limitations
		1. all of these trials lacked a control group, and very few studies implemented a controlled feeding protocol
		2. sample sizes of the various study arms were quite small (n=7–19)
		3. the studies may have lacked power to detect significant differences within or between groups. In view of these limitations.
