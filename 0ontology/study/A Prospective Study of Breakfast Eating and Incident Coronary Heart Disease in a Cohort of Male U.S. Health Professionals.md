# Findings
[[behavior_breakfast_omission|Breakfast skipping]]
- Skipping breakfast was associated with a **27% higher risk of coronary heart disease (CHD)**  
- This association was **mediated by**:
    - [[metric_body_mass_index|BMI]]
    - [[condition_hypertension|hypertension]]
    - [[condition_hypercholesterolemia|hypercholesterolemia]]
    - [[diabetes]]

[[behavior_late_night_eating|Late-night eating]]
- Eating late at night was associated with a **55% higher risk of CHD** 
- This association was also **mediated by**:
    - [[metric_body_mass_index|BMI]]
    - [[condition_hypertension|hypertension]]
    - [[condition_hypercholesterolemia|hypercholesterolemia]]
    - [[diabetes]]

[[behavior_meal_frequency|Eating frequency]]
- **No association** was observed between eating frequency (meals per day) and CHD risk.
