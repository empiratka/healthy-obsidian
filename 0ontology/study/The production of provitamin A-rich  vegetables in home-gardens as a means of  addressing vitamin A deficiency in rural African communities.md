# Main Findings
- Vitamin A deficiency impairs the immune system
- Vitamin A is also needed for maintaining eye health and vision. The earliest ocular sign of vitamin A deficiency is night blindness. This can progress to structural eye damage. This may eventually result in impairment of vision or irreversible blindness
- Vitamin A in the diet is available from animal sources in the form of preformed vitamin A or retinol and from plant sources, particularly vegetables and fruits, in the form of provitamin A carotenoids (predominantly β-carotene). Provitamin A carotenoids in vegetables and fruits achieve vitamin A activity when they are converted to retinol in the body
