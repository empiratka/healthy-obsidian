**Non-essential bioactive compounds** are dietary constituents that are not required for survival and do not prevent deficiency diseases, but can interact with biological systems to influence health outcomes.
# Properties
- No established dietary requirement (RDA / AI)
- Absence does not cause deficiency disease
- Presence may:
    - modulate metabolic pathways
    - reduce disease risk
    - support healthspan or longevity

These compounds are central to modern research in preventive nutrition and longevity science.
